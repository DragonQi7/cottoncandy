📋 Good to Know Before You Arrive

  • 🍽️Eat before you come — please have a light meal before arriving so the kids aren't hungry on the rides.
  • ⏰Arrive 10–15 minutes early — party starts at 12:00 PM sharp, so aim to be at the Birthday Party Podium by 11:45 AM.
  • 🎢Rides from 12:00 – 2:20 PM — kids explore Nickelodeon Universe with chaperones on hand the whole time.
  • 🍕Mealtime at 2:20 PM, cake at 2:40 PM — pizza, drinks, and birthday cake in the private party room!

🚗 Parking & Arrival

Please arrive 10–15 minutes before 12:00 PM

  • 🅿️Park in Parking Deck C at American Dream Mall.
  • 🚶Enter the building and head to the 1st Floor. Walk toward Victoria's Secret, then continue past the Ice Rink.
  • 🎯Keep walking straight until you reach Nickelodeon Universe. Go to the Birthday Party Podium to the left of the Slime Tower.
  • 🎉Check in at the podium — your Party Host will meet you there and escort the group to the party area!
